Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

What we collect

Free Peptide University is a static educational website. When you enter your email address to unlock member content, that unlock currently happens entirely in your own browser (using local storage on your device). Your reading progress and unlock state are stored on your device only.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this site.
  • We do not require an account to read the public library.

Community membership

Our private community is hosted on Skool. If you request to join, the information you submit there is handled by Skool under Skool's privacy policy, and is visible to the community's administrators for reviewing your request.

Email

If and when we begin sending an email newsletter, it will be opt-in, every message will include an unsubscribe link, and this policy will be updated to name the email provider we use.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log standard technical data (such as your IP address) when the font files are requested. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard web-server request data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the community page.
