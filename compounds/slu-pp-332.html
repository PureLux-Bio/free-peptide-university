SLU-PP-332

An ERRα agonist studied as an exercise-mimetic

Metabolic researchEvidence: preclinicalRUO · not approved
Research Use Only. Not an approved medicine and not for human use. Educational only — no dosing or medical advice.

What it is

SLU-PP-332 is a small molecule (not a peptide) studied as an agonist of the estrogen-related receptor α (ERRα), marketed in research circles as an "exercise mimetic."

Proposed mechanism

ERRα regulates mitochondrial and oxidative-metabolism genes; agonizing it is proposed to increase fat oxidation and endurance in rodents without exercise.1 This is preclinical.

State of the evidence

  • Rodent data only; no human trials.

Regulatory & status

  • Not approved; RUO.
